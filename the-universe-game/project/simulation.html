Synopsis

The Universe
Game

In an old, dying universe, a lonely, intellectually isolated super-advanced life form wanders the cosmos in search of purpose, understanding and companionship.

The life form happens upon an ancient advanced game, The Universe Game, built by extinct architects exclusively for highly intelligent species. The game dissolves players into a primordial soup and merges them directly with the game computer itself, powered by a black hole.

The objective of the game is to steer intelligent life forms inside of a fully rendered universe simulation towards Ascension, or superintelligence. Through trial and error, the "player" happens upon a species that captures their hearts and imagination: humanity. The player believes they must steer humanity towards Ascension through nurture and care.

They later learn that there are other players with ulterior motives and wildly differing philosophies, such as "survival of the fittest", that should be employed when steering a species to Ascension.

As tensions begin to rise, The Player learns that The Universe Game is far more sinister than its premise suggests, and that they were being manipulated. As the layers of the game are peeled back, the true antagonist, and plot, are revealed.
